# How to play

## Install
**From the competition link (recommended):** open the invite link from the Devpost page, join the "Competition" release channel, then choose **Install on headset**. The Last Frequency appears in your Quest library.

**From this pack (sideload):** with developer mode on and the headset connected by USB:
```
adb install -r build/LastFrequency_v5.apk
```
Then open it from **Library > Unknown Sources > The Last Frequency**.

## Before you start
- **Sit down.** Everything is built for a chair; you never need to stand or turn around quickly.
- **Put the controllers away.** The app is hands only. Hold your hands up and the headset switches to hand tracking.
- **Allow the microphone** when asked. (If you say no, the song still leads the world; you just won't steer it with your voice.)
- A quiet room helps the pitch tracking; any voice works, trained or not.

## The 3-minute route
1. **The dark.** You start in a dark room lit only by your own fingertips. Look at your hands; that glow is you.
2. **Poke SING** (the wide glowing button at the top of the menu, under "Start here").
3. **Pick a voice style** in the studio, then poke **START THE SHOW**.
4. **Sing along.** Lyrics float in front of you. Watch what happens:
   - wonder-buds drift within reach; **touch one** and it blooms with a note;
   - **hold a note** and the spiral staircase climbs higher;
   - **sing high** for bright, quick magic; **sing low** for deep, slow magic;
   - **raise both hands above your head** to conduct a burst of light;
   - **pinch and draw** in the air: a **rectangle** opens a door of light, a **circle** opens a halo of snowflakes, a **triangle** raises crystal spires.
5. **The finale.** "THE WORLD IS YOURS. You sang it into being."
6. **Keep it.** Poke **SAVE MY SONG**, then **SHARE TO PHONE** and scan the QR code with your phone on the same Wi-Fi.

## Then try
- **WORLD** button: switch to **Jungle Temple** and its song "Wake the Green". Push a hand outward to raise land and trees, lift a hand to pour a waterfall, push with both hands to grow stairs toward the stone guardian, and climb them.
- **COMPOSE YOUR OWN:** type a word; it becomes a key, a tempo and six instruments you play by touch.
- **LISTEN AND BUILD:** play any music near the headset and the world builds from how it feels.
- **SHOW ME THE FLOW:** a ghost hand walks you through every button.
- **OPTIONS:** mixed reality (your room becomes the stage), calm mode, no-microphone mode.

## If something feels off
- Menu not in view: look around; it appears in front of where you were facing when it opened.
- Hands not responding: put the controllers down and hold your hands in good light.
- No reaction to your voice: check the microphone permission in the headset's Settings > Privacy.
