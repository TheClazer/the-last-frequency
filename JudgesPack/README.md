## The feeling we are building

Most of us stopped singing out loud a long time ago. Somewhere along the way we learned that singing is for people who are "good at it", and the rest of us went quiet.

The Last Frequency is our answer to that silence. It is a place where your voice is not judged; it is **needed**. Hum, and a bud blooms. Hold a note, and a staircase climbs into the aurora. Sing high and the magic turns bright and quick; sing low and it turns deep and slow. Every single sound you make leaves something behind.

People who say "I can't sing" are the people we built this for.

## In one table

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A seated VR musical: sing along to an original song while the 360° world builds around you from your voice and hands |
| **Worlds** | Aurora Ice (default) and Jungle Temple, each with its own original song |
| **Hands** | Poke buttons with a fingertip; draw shapes in the air (rectangle, circle, triangle) to cast magic; push, lift and climb in the jungle |
| **Voice** | Real-time pitch tracking on the headset, about 73 Hz to 1.3 kHz: low voices and high voices both work |
| **More ways in** | COMPOSE (a typed word becomes a song you play by touch), LISTEN AND BUILD (the world reacts to music in your room), mixed reality, calm mode, no-mic mode |
| **Keep it** | SAVE MY SONG mixes your voice and the music into a WAV; SHARE TO PHONE opens it on your phone by QR code, over your own Wi-Fi, nothing uploaded |
| **Measured on a Quest 3S** | Steady 72 fps (59 seconds at 72, 57 at 73, 3 at 71 in a full on-device run); mixed reality passthrough working; microphone and both hands detected |
