The Last Frequency: privacy policy (draft, host as a static page)

What the app uses. The microphone, to detect your singing pitch and loudness and, in Listen mode, the loudness and rhythm of music playing in the room. Hand tracking, to let you control the app with your hands. Passthrough (optional, off by default), to show your room inside the experience.

What it stores. On your headset only: a WAV of your song when you press SAVE MY SONG (your microphone take mixed with the music), a WAV of a composition when you press SEND TO STUDIO, and, after each song, a small performance file (head and fingertip positions, voice loudness and pitch) in the app's own folder. Your voice is held in memory during a song and is written to disk only when you save.

What it sends. The app itself sends nothing off your headset. The Meta Quest platform and the Meta XR SDK the app is built on may collect their own diagnostic and usage data under Meta's privacy policy (https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/); this app does not control or receive that data. Audio is analysed on the device and is never uploaded. "Share to phone" runs a temporary web server on your own Wi-Fi network, open only while the share screen is showing; anyone on that network who has the address can see your saved files until you close it.

Camera images. The app does not store or transmit passthrough camera images.

Children. The app is intended for users aged 13 and over.
