The Last Frequency: Privacy Policy

What the app uses. The microphone, to detect your singing pitch and loudness and, in Listen mode, the loudness and rhythm of music playing in the room. Hand tracking, to let you control the app with your hands. Passthrough (optional), to show your room inside the experience.

What it stores. Recordings you choose to save (a WAV of your song and a performance file) are kept on your headset only.

What it sends. Nothing leaves your headset. Audio is analysed on the device and is never uploaded. "Share to phone" runs a temporary web server on your own Wi-Fi network, open only while the share screen is showing; anyone on that network with the address can see your saved files until you close it.

Camera images. The app does not store or transmit passthrough camera images.

Children. The app is intended for users aged 13 and over.
